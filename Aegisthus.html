AEGISTHUS

Aegisthus was born from an incestous union between Thyestes and his daughter Pelopia, making her both his mother and sister and Thyestes his father and Grandfather. Thyestes did this after receving advice from an oracle that said the son born from the union would be the one to kill his brother Atreus, allowing Thyestes to finally become king of Mycenae. When she was raped Pelopia took the sword from the man who did so, upon realising it was her fathers Pelopia abandoned Aegisthus with the sword and killed herself out of shame. Atreus was the one who found Aegisthus and, not knowing his origin, took him in and raised him so that he might one day kill Thyestes. When he was older Atreus sent Aegisthus to kill Thyestes, however Aegisthus was carrying Thyestes' sword, so when he arrived he was recognised and Thyestes claimed him as his son. Reunited with his father, Aegisthus and Thystes marched to Mycenae where Aegisthus killed Atreus (the man who raised him) and chased Atreus' two sons, Agamemnon and Menelaus, out of Mycenae. After this Thystes and Aegisthus ruled Mycenae together, that is until Agamemnon and Menelaus returned with the forces of Sparta, whose royal family they had married into, and took back the throne. Out of mercy though Agamemnon and Menelaus didn't kill Thyestes and Aegisthus but exiled them. Agamemnon ruled Mycenae after this, however when Agamemnon and Menelaus left to fight in the Trojan war Aegisthus returned.

Aegisthus during and after the Trojan war

When Aegisthus returned to Mycenae while Agamemnon and Menelaus were at war he began an affair with Agamemnon's wife, Clytemnestra, who had turned against him after he scarificed their eldest child Iphigenia. The two were together for the 10 years it took to win the trojan war ruling over Mycenae together. During this time the two abuse Clytemnestra's children by Agamemnon and banish Orestes, Clytemnestra and Agamemnon's only son and heir to the throne. When Agamemnon returned, the two plotted to kill him. Their are several versions of how Agamemnon dies the two most popular being the version where Clytemnestra alone kills Agamemnon while he bathes, and the version where Aegisthus kills Agamemnon at a feast held in his honour which was really a distraction to do so. After killing Agamemnon, Clytemnestra and Aegisthus ruled Mycenae together for 7 years. Their reign was interrupted in the eigth year by the return of Orestes, who killed both Clytemnestra and Aegisthus for the murder of Agamemnon.

Facts about Aegisthus

  • Aegisthus is pronouced ee-jis-thuhus. The ee ryhmes with see and the second syllable jis is stressed.
  • Aegisthus' name means male goat becuse he was suckled by a goat after his abandonment in infancy.
